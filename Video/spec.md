# Spec

The task is to create an 8 minute video essay about a certain algorithm, in this case we have chosen Strassen's algorithm for matrix multiplicatino.

The visuals should be split 30% time spent in the IDE (for the code walkthrough component) 60% in a powerpoint presentation with visual aids and 10% should be a custom manim animation for a tricky component.

For the topic breakdown, see the below:

```
Research your chosen topic (Wikipedia is an okay start, but don't stop there, please add two extra custom sections that show extra research has been undertaken). Collate information addressing the following areas:

    What problem does the algorithm solve?
    How does the algorithm function?
    What are the constraints on the application of the algorithm (cases where it's valid or invalid, solution precision, etc. as appropriate)?
    What is the theoretical complexity of the algorithm? To be accompanied by an idea of why it has this complexity.
    What are the practical limitations (if any) of the algorithm?
    Is there anything else interesting about it?

These areas are not equally weighted. The heart of the video is how the algorithm functions and why its complexity is what it is — spend most of your time there. The remaining areas can each be dealt with in a sentence or two; a video that touches all six areas superficially will score worse than one that explains the mechanism well and covers the rest briefly.
```
